[1] Sharma et al. 2020
In a review of 18 trials, regular aerobic exercise improved sleep quality in young adults. The improvement was largest when exercise was performed for at least eight weeks.

[2] Patel 2019
University students who exercised three times per week fell asleep 12 minutes faster than inactive students. Total sleep time was similar between groups.

[3] Gupta & Rao 2021
In this small pilot study of 20 adults, evening exercise may slightly delay sleep onset for some participants. For most participants the effect was not significant.

[4] Kulkarni 2018
Among 300 university students, screen use before bedtime reduced total sleep duration by an average of 25 minutes per night.

[6] Fernandes 2017
Mindfulness training improved self-reported sleep in hospital nurses. The study did not include students.

[7] Nair 2020
Students with more daylight exposure showed earlier and more regular sleep timing.
